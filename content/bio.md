I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate at the [State Key Laboratory of Internet of Things for Smart City (SKL-IOTSC)](https://skliotsc.um.edu.mo/), [University of Macau](https://www.um.edu.mo/), advised by [Prof. Li Li](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/personal/llili/) and [Prof. Chengzhong Xu (IEEE Fellow)](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/personal/czxu/).

I am currently a Visiting Scholar at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), working with [Prof. Wei Yang Bryan Lim](https://sites.google.com/view/wyb/people). I also maintain a long-term research collaboration with [Prof. Zhijiang Guo](https://cartus.github.io/).

My research focuses on three closely connected directions:

- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** efficient adaptation and knowledge reuse.
- **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs):** hallucination mitigation and universal multimodal embeddings.
- **Agents:** automated research and search agents.

I welcome research discussions and collaboration. Please feel free to reach out by email.
