I am a PhD student at **SKL-IOTSC, University of Macau**, supervised by [Prof. Li Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=uLzU3OcAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Cheng-Zhong Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=XsBBTUgAAAAJ&hl=en). I am also fortunate to maintain a long-term research collaboration with [Prof. Zhijiang Guo](https://cartus.github.io/) and to visit [Prof. Wei Yang Bryan Lim](https://sites.google.com/view/wyb/people) at Nanyang Technological University.

My research focuses on two closely connected directions:

- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** efficient adaptation, including memory-efficient fine-tuning and knowledge reuse.
- **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs):** hallucination mitigation and universal multimodal embeddings.

I welcome research discussions and collaboration. Please feel free to reach out by email.
