I am a PhD student at the **State Key Laboratory of Internet of Things for Smart City, University of Macau**. My research focuses on edge computing, large language models, and multimodal large language models.

My current work explores three closely connected directions:

- **Efficient model adaptation:** memory-efficient fine-tuning, knowledge reuse, and heterogeneous collaboration for on-device and federated scenarios.
- **Trustworthy multimodal models:** mitigating MLLM hallucinations through attention intervention, contrastive decoding, and agentic debate.
- **Universal multimodal representations:** scalable task modeling and hard-negative mining for general-purpose multimodal embeddings.

I welcome research discussions and collaboration. Please feel free to reach out by email.
